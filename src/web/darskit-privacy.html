Privacy policy

This policy describes the current version of DarsKit.

Information processed

DarsKit processes the lesson details, source text, and supported files that you choose to provide while using the app. It also interacts with the Canva design you opened so it can add generated educational content.

Storage and external accounts

The current version does not require a separate DarsKit account and does not intentionally store uploaded lesson files, lesson text, or generated teaching content on a Ghaith Web server. File parsing and document generation are performed within the app session, and generated content is added to your Canva design.

Canva data

Your use of Canva remains subject to Canva's own terms and privacy practices. DarsKit requests only the Canva capabilities needed to perform its visible features.

Children and student data

DarsKit is intended as a teacher productivity tool. Do not upload unnecessary personal or sensitive student information. Teachers remain responsible for reviewing materials and following applicable school and privacy requirements.

Changes

If a future version introduces server-side storage, authentication, analytics, or premium backend features, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
